How to practise / Cómo practicar

Listen to a word or sentence, then say it. If the browser recognises your answer, it moves to the next exercise. If not, listen and try again. / Escucha y repite; si el navegador reconoce la respuesta, continúa.

Speech recognition checks words, not the accuracy of each English sound. The teacher should assess difficult endings and word stress. Microphone speech recognition may send audio to your browser provider. This page does not store audio, names or results on a server. Desktop Chrome/Edge is recommended.
