How to practise / Cómo practicar

1. Click Listen. 2. For past-tense verbs, choose /t/, /d/ or /ɪd/ after listening. 3. Click Speak now and repeat the word. 4. If the browser recognises the expected expression, move automatically to the next item. If not, listen and try again.

Escucha, identifica el sonido cuando sea necesario, y repite. Si el navegador reconoce la palabra, avanzas automáticamente. Si no la reconoce, vuelve a intentarlo.

Important: Speech recognition is not a phoneme-level pronunciation assessment. It can recognise a word even when its ending is incorrect. The /t/, /d/, /ɪd/ listening choice is checked separately, but the application cannot confirm that the student actually produced that ending. Teachers should listen to the student's pronunciation when checking fine sound distinctions. Browser speech recognition can process audio through an external service. No recordings or student names are saved by this site.
